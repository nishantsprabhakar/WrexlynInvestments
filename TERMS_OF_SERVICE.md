## 1. What Wrexlyn for Investments Is

Wrexlyn for Investments is a locally-installed private-equity and venture-capital deal
intelligence platform. It uses third-party large language models ("LLMs") to screen companies,
draft investment committee notes and financial models, review documents for risk, and otherwise
analyze deal material you provide, and it maintains a local, structured record of deals,
portfolio companies, valuations, cap tables, and investment-committee decisions on the machine
where it is installed. It is provided as-is, free of charge unless a separate written agreement

## 3. AI-Generated and Computed Output — No Guarantee of Correctness

Much of what the Software produces — company screening ratings, investment theses, risk
narratives, redline suggestions, and IC memorandum sections — is generated by a third-party LLM.
**LLMs make mistakes, including mistakes that look confident and plausible**, and can
misread, omit, or fabricate details from the documents you provide. Separately, the Software
computes certain figures deterministically in code rather than asking the LLM for them (for
example EBITDA margins, growth rates, IRR/MOIC, DCF valuations, and cap-table dilution) — this
reduces but does not eliminate risk, since a deterministic calculation is only as good as the
inputs it was given, some of which may themselves be LLM-extracted from a deck or model. **You
are solely responsible for independently reviewing, verifying, and validating every figure,
claim, and document the Software generates or computes before using it to inform an investment,
divestment, follow-on, valuation, IC decision, or any other decision involving real capital.**
Any classification the Software assigns to a claim (for example "sourced fact" versus "AI
interpretation" versus "unverified assertion") is a best-effort labeling aid, not a guarantee that
the label is correct or that the underlying claim is true.
